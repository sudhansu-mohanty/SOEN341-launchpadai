## Project Description</br>
LaunchpadAI is a web-based platform designed to help job seekers manage their job search activities. The system allows users to create profiles, upload and manage resumes, search for job opportunities, track submitted applications, and follow the progress of their application process. The platform aims to centralize job-search activities and help users stay organized throughout their career development journey.

## Identified problem </br>
Job searching means juggling countless postings across scattered platforms, each with its own application format. Beyond the initial search, tracking multiple applications' statuses, deadlines, and follow-ups becomes its own challenge, making an already stressful process feel even more chaotic and demotivating without a clear system.

## Proposed solution </br>
Merging resume management, job discovery, and application tracking into a single platform eliminates the need to juggle multiple tools, tabs, and spreadsheets. Users gain a centralized view of their entire job search journey, thus reducing cognitive load, minimizing missed deadlines, and replacing scattered chaos with organized clarity and confidence.
